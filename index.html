A letter that waited for you

Sneha

♡
Some feelings are too beautiful
to fit inside a conversation.
To the girl I keep choosing

My Love,

Sometimes I wonder how a person can become such a large part of another person's world without even being there.

You did.

Somewhere between our conversations, your laughter, your little habits, the things you tell me, the things you don't, and all those ordinary moments that somehow became important to me, you quietly became someone I cannot imagine my life without.

If I could trade every beautiful thing I have ever seen, I would gladly give it all away for one simple minute beside you.

Just one minute.

A minute to hear your voice without a phone between us. A minute to watch you laugh and know that I am the reason for it. A minute to look into your eyes and forget whatever else exists around me.

I would want to remember your face exactly as it is in that moment — your eyes, your hair, your lips, your smile, everything that makes you you.

But strangely, as much as I love all of those things, they are not what I love most about you.

I love your mind.

That endlessly surprising place inside you that I keep discovering piece by piece. Your thoughts, your opinions, your strange little ideas, the way you see things differently, the things that make you laugh, the things that hurt you, and the dreams you carry quietly inside yourself.

There are parts of you I understand, and there are parts I still don't.

And I think that's one of the things I love most.

What I hope you always remember
You are not a story
I want to finish reading.

You are a story
I want to keep discovering.

Every conversation feels like another page. Every new thing I learn about you feels like finding a hidden room in a house I thought I already knew.

And somehow, the more I discover, the more I want to know.

I don't only want your happiest days. I want to know you on the days when you are quiet. On the days when you don't feel like talking. On the days when everything feels too much.

I want to know what makes you afraid, what makes you hopeful, what you secretly wish for, and what you never say out loud.

I want to know the person behind the smile.

Because loving someone, to me, isn't simply admiring the beautiful parts of them.

It is wanting to understand all of them.

There are moments when I wish distance were something I could simply argue with.

Because there are times when all I want is to reach for your hand and realize that you're actually there.

No screen.
No typing.
No waiting for a message.

Just you.
And me.

Sitting beside each other, perhaps saying nothing at all. I think I would be happy even then.

Because with the right person, silence doesn't feel empty.

It feels like home.

And if someday I get that ordinary minute with you, I don't think I'll ask for anything extraordinary.

I won't need some perfect place or some grand occasion. I'll just want you beside me.

Maybe I'll look at you for a little too long. Maybe you'll ask me, "What?" And I'll probably smile and say, "Nothing."

But the truth will be that I'm trying to memorize the moment.

Because I've spent so much time missing you that I think I would want to remember every second of finally having you close.

Your voice.
Your laugh.
Your eyes.
Your hands.
Your smile.
Your presence.

And most of all...
you.

If life gives me the choice between a thousand magnificent moments without you and one imperfect, ordinary moment with you, I already know what I would choose.

I would choose
the ordinary moment.

I would choose the conversation that lasts too long. The laugh that comes at the wrong time. The silence after we've run out of things to say. The little arguments that end with us finding our way back to each other.

I would choose all of it.

Because I don't want a perfect love story.

I want ours.

The complicated parts. The beautiful parts. The unexpected parts. The memories we haven't made yet.

I don't know what the future has written for us. But I know what I would like to write into it.

More mornings where I get to hear your voice. More nights where I don't want the conversation to end. More photographs. More silly arguments. More laughter. More moments where we look at each other and realize how far we've come.

And someday, hopefully,
fewer goodbyes.

I don't promise that I will always know exactly what to say. I don't promise that I'll always get everything right.

But I can promise you this:

I will keep choosing you.

Not only when everything is easy. Not only when we are happy. But also when things become difficult, when distance feels heavy, when words fail us, and when loving each other requires patience.

Because you are not simply someone I fell in love with.

You are someone I continue to discover.

And perhaps that's why my love for you doesn't feel like something that happened once.

It feels like something that happens again and again.

Every time you laugh.
Every time you say my name.
Every time you tell me something you have never told anyone else.
Every time I see your face.
Every time I realize that, somehow, after everything, you are still the person I want to tell everything to.

So if you ever wonder what I want from you, the answer is surprisingly simple.

I don't need perfection.
I don't need grand promises.
I don't need every day to be extraordinary.

I just want your presence in my life.

I want to keep knowing you.
Keep loving you.
Keep discovering you.

And if I am lucky enough to spend a lifetime doing that, I think I will still reach the end feeling as though I have only just begun.

Because you are not someone I want to have only for a moment.

You are someone I want to keep choosing for all the moments that come after it.
♡

If I had to choose again...
I would still choose you.

Again.
And again.
And again.

With all my love,
Anand ♡
